# Dojo Deckbuilder v2.0 Alpha — Reversal Prototype

## Decision

**Reversal** is Paper-Fu’s answer to an UNO-style reverse: when a fighter successfully Blocks an Attack with a Defense card, the attacker has created an opening and the defender may immediately hit back.

Reversal is a bold **core combat rule**, not a rare card-specific trick. It makes every successful Defense a possible momentum swing while still requiring the defender to have an Attack in hand and survive the counterattack that follows.

## Rules text

> **Reversal** — Once per round, after a Defense card you play Blocks an Attack, you may immediately play one Attack from your hand against that Attack’s controller.
>
> Resolve that Attack normally. It does not count against your normal Attack limit. The original attacker may play a Defense normally.
>
> A Reversal Attack cannot cause another Reversal.

## Rules consequences

- A Defense card must be played. Passive Character DEF, Armor, or another player’s Defense alone cannot create your Reversal.
- “Blocks” means the original Attack deals 0 combat Damage. No separate **full Block** term is needed.
- The Reversal Attack uses normal Attack rules: its own card Damage, the defender’s ATK, Weapons, Tempo, zone, modifiers, and a normal Reaction Window.
- A Reversal Attack is made during another player’s turn. It gains normal Attack XP, but does not generate Focus because it was not played during your own turn.
- The Reversal Attack does not count against your normal two-Attack limit and cannot trigger or advance a learned Combo unless that Combo expressly permits an Attack made as a Reversal.
- The original attacker may defend normally, but their Defense cannot create another Reversal. The action then returns to the original player’s turn.
- A Reversal may target a Boss after a player Blocks a Boss Attack. In Tag Team, it targets the original attacking active fighter.

## Example

Rita attacks Devin with a High strike. Devin plays a High Defense card and the Attack is Blocked. Devin has not used Reversal this round, so he immediately plays a Low Kick from his hand against Rita. The Kick uses Devin’s ATK, eligible Weapon bonuses, and Tempo normally. Rita may answer with a legal Low Defense. After that strike fully resolves, Rita’s original turn continues.

## Balance guardrails

- One Reversal per player per round; mark it with the player’s existing Tempo token flipped to its reverse side, so the Alpha adds no new tracker.
- Reversal is optional. A player may save an Attack for their own turn instead.
- Reversal cannot chain, trigger an extra Reversal, or count toward existing Combo sequences by default.
- Test first in Quick Duel, Tag Team, and Boss Blitz for stall patterns, defensive dominance, and Boss pacing.

## Deliberately rejected for core

### Literal attack redirection

Making the original Attack target its controller is funny but creates ambiguity about controller, target, attack text, equipment, and Boss behavior. It may appear on a one-off expansion card later, not as the core rule.

### Mirror every effect

Reversing all discard, Exhaust, Stun, and other penalties would create an exception factory. Those belong on individual cards, not in Reversal’s base definition.
